What is Planetclaire

Planetclaire is a Düsseldorf-based event series built around carefully curated nights — where the music, the room and the crowd matter equally.

From intimate bunker nights to open dancefloors, every Planetclaire event is curated with intention. No headliner worship, no genre dogma — just a room, a sound system, and a night that goes where it needs to go.

Resident DJs and selected guests share the booth across the night, keeping the energy honest from first record to last.
